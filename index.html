Questions

Before you register

Do I send my documents to Drive on InDrive?

No. You register yourself in the official inDrive app and enter your details there. The inDrive support team handles your documents directly.

Is it really free?

Yes. Registration is free with no hidden fees. Official scouts are paid directly by inDrive.

Which store do I download from?

Use the download button on this page. It sends Android phones to Google Play and iPhones to the App Store.

Download the inDrive app
My driver's license has expired. Can I still register?

Yes, bring a GPT form together with your expired license. Your vehicle registration and road worthiness certificates must still be valid.

I don't own a car. Can you provide one?

No. We do not offer work-and-pay cars, and you need your own vehicle to register.
