Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Drive on inDrive ("we", "us", "our") helps drivers in Lagos and Abuja, Nigeria register on the inDrive platform. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By submitting your details to us, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need to contact you and help you register. This includes:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Any details you choose to send us when you message us (for example, your city or the type of vehicle you drive)

You may give us this information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form shown on our ads, through our website, or by messaging us directly.

2. Information From Meta Lead Forms

When you click one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram and fill in an instant form, the details you enter (such as your full name and phone number) are collected through Meta's lead form tools and passed to us. Meta processes information in line with its own privacy policy, which you can read at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you by phone call, SMS, WhatsApp or other messaging about registering as a driver on inDrive
  • To guide you through the registration process until you complete your first trip
  • To answer your questions and follow up on your enquiry
  • To improve our service and measure how well our advertising works

We do not use your information for any purpose unrelated to the above without asking you first.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • inDrive, where needed to process your driver registration
  • Service providers that help us run our business (such as Meta, our website host and messaging tools), only as needed to provide those services
  • Authorities, where we are required to do so by law

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information only for as long as needed to follow up on your enquiry and complete your registration, or as required by law. After that, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

6. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including limiting access to the people who need it to help you. No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

In line with applicable data protection laws in Nigeria, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Withdraw your consent or ask us to stop contacting you at any time

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 10 and we will respond as soon as reasonably possible.

8. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website and advertising may use cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure and improve our ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are for adults who are legally allowed to drive. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.
